# Reach

Delulu is for young women who want an AI they can talk to honestly about decisions, and who do not want an AI company or an app company holding that diary.

Arkiv helps them in one place: when they change phones or clear the browser, their encrypted diary comes back with one tap, with no account on our side and no database of their words.

We have no usage numbers yet. This is a hackathon build. The reach we aim for: people who already use Telegram or a web app on a phone, sign in with email or Google (an embedded wallet is made for them, no seed phrase), and never need to know that Arkiv exists.
